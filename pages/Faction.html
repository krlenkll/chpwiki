SOP 2.01 — Patrol Operations

Policy Number: CHP-SOP-2.01

Division: Patrol Operations

Version: 1.0

1. Purpose

This section establishes general operational guidelines for personnel conducting patrol duties.

2. Policy

Personnel shall conduct patrol activities in accordance with applicable law and department policy.

3. Procedures

3.1 Patrol Preparation

Personnel should ensure that required equipment, communications systems and assigned vehicles are operational before beginning patrol.

3.2 Field Operations

Personnel should maintain situational awareness, follow communication procedures and document significant incidents as required.

4. Documentation

Required reports shall be completed in accordance with applicable department procedures.
